The idea

Theme pages share content around one clear topic instead of one person's life. I picked travel because it's visual, it has a huge audience, and travel brands and creators are always looking for exposure.

How I built it

  1. Started the page. Set up a travel-focused account with a consistent look and posting style.
  2. Grew the audience. Posted consistently and grew the page past 20,000 followers.
  3. Used the numbers. Tracked engagement analytics to see which posts worked and adjusted the content mix.
  4. Monetized it. Coordinated with other creators to sell ad placements and shoutouts.

What it shows

  • Marketing analytics: reading engagement data and acting on it
  • Entrepreneurship: turning an audience into revenue
  • Negotiation and coordination: working out deals with other creators
