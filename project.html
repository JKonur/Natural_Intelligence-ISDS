What I did

  1. Started flying at 13 (2019). Learned to fly and shoot with a DJI Mavic 2 Pro, capturing aerial photo and video wherever I traveled, from Colorado to Costa Rica.
  2. Built a library. Over the next couple of years I collected a large set of shots across several states and countries.
  3. Put it to work (around 2021). Listed the content on Pond5 and Shutterstock and sold more than 80 licenses.
  4. Found repeat use. My footage has been licensed for use on many travel and tourism websites.
  5. Took on clients. Shot commercial aerial content as a freelance contractor, including a stadium shoot for Destrehan High School.

What it taught me

  • Even as a teenager, I found that people valued my work once I put it out there.
  • Online marketplaces can put your work in front of buyers you would never meet otherwise.
  • Shooting for a client means working to what they need, not just what I want to shoot.
